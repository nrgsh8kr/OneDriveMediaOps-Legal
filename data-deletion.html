Data Deletion

OneDrive Photos & Videos Dupes (also listed as OneDrive MediaOps) · applies to app version 1.1.48 and earlier

In short: the app does not create an account, and your photos, videos and file contents are never sent to us. Duplicate detection runs on your device. The only data that leaves your device is optional analytics and crash reports sent to Google Firebase, which are on by default and which you can turn off in the app.

What data exists, and where

None of this is linked to your name, email address or Microsoft account.

How to delete your data

1. Data on your device

  • Everything: uninstall the app, or on Android go to Settings → Apps → OneDrive Photos & Videos Dupes → Storage & cache → Clear storage.
  • Your Microsoft sign-in only: tap Sign Out on the app's main screen.

2. Stop sending analytics and crash reports

Turn off the Analytics & Crash Reports switch in the Privacy section at the bottom of the scan settings screen (shown after you choose folders to scan). Nothing further is sent while it is off. Data already sent is not recalled by switching it off.

3. Analytics and crash data already sent

Current limitation (version 1.1.48 and earlier): these records are identified only by random IDs that the app does not show you, not by your name or email. That means we cannot find one person's records from an email address, so we cannot currently delete them on request. Uninstalling the app resets the IDs, so records sent before that are no longer linked to your device, and Firebase removes them at the end of its retention period.

An upcoming version adds a way to copy your diagnostic ID in the app, so that you can send it to us and we can delete the matching records, and a button to reset the ID and delete analytics data still on your device. This page will be updated when it is released.
