Data Deletion

OneDrive Photos & Videos Dupes (also listed as OneDrive MediaOps) · app version 1.1.49 and later (older versions: see below)

In short: the app does not create an account, and your photos, videos and file contents are never sent to us. Duplicate detection runs on your device. The only data that leaves your device is optional analytics and crash reports sent to Google Firebase, which are on by default and which you can turn off in the app.

How to delete your data

1. Data on your device

  • Everything: uninstall the app, or on Android go to Settings → Apps → OneDrive Photos & Videos Dupes → Storage & cache → Clear storage.
  • Your Microsoft sign-in only: tap Sign Out on the app's main screen.

2. Stop sending analytics and crash reports

Turn off the Analytics & Crash Reports switch in the Privacy section at the bottom of the scan settings screen (shown after you choose folders to scan). Nothing further is sent while it is off. Data already sent is not recalled by switching it off.

3. Delete analytics data on your device

In the Privacy section of the scan settings screen, tap Delete my analytics data. This turns analytics and crash reports off, deletes analytics data still on your device, and resets your diagnostic ID. It does not remove data already sent; for that, see step 4 before you tap it, because the ID changes.

4. Delete analytics and crash data already sent

  1. In the same Privacy section, tap Copy diagnostic ID.
  2. Email the ID to us (address below) and ask for deletion.

We then delete the analytics data and crash reports that carry that ID: from Google Analytics, from Google Firebase Crashlytics (Google completes this within 30 days of our request), and from our BigQuery copies.

App versions before 1.1.49: data sent by those versions is identified only by random IDs the app never showed, not by your name or email, so it cannot be matched to you or deleted on request. Updating to 1.1.49 or later, then using the steps above, covers data sent from then on. Uninstalling resets the IDs, and Firebase removes older records at the end of its retention period.
